I am Professor of Artificial Intelligence in Remote Sensing at [Hochschule für Technik Stuttgart](https://www.hft-stuttgart.de) since 2024.

Previously, I was Assistant Professor of Computer Vision (2022 - 2024) at the [School of Computer
Science](https://www.unisg.ch/en/universitaet/schools/school-of-computer-science/) and Senior Scientist (2020 - 2022) at the [AIML Lab](https://hsg-aiml.github.io/) of the [Universität St. Gallen](https://www.unisg.ch/en/) (Switzerland), where I gained experience in deep learning and machine learning.

Before that, my research focused on a completely different field: solar system astronomy. As a postdoc and associate researcher at [Northern Arizona University](https://nau.edu/) (2013 - 2018) and as an astronomer at [Lowell Observatory](https://lowell.edu/) (2018 - 2020), I used ground-based and space-based telescopic observations as well as numerical models to study the physical properties of asteroids and comets, and developed scientific software.

Before becoming an astronomer, I earned my doctorate in geosciences at [Freie Universität Berlin](https://www.fu-berlin.de/) after completing my [dissertation project](https://refubium.fu-berlin.de/bitstream/handle/fub188/2283/PhD_mommert_fuonline.pdf?sequence=1&isAllowed=y) at the [Deutschen Zentrum für Luft- und Raumfahrt (DLR)](https://www.dlr.de/de/pf/) (2009 - 2013). My academic career began with a diploma in physics, which I completed at [Universität Heidelberg](https://www.uni-heidelberg.de/) (2004 - 2009).
